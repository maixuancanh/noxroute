Public recurring swaps turn strategy into a target.

Transparent calldata and token transfers let observers infer trading direction, recurring cadence, remaining budget and sensitive limits.

  • Wallet-level trackers can follow repeated clips.
  • Plain limits expose where a strategy will stop.
  • Small private batches reveal intent when matching happens too late.
02 / The Nox route

Encrypt the intent, settle only the remainder.

NoxRoute makes Nox load-bearing: strategy rules become encrypted handles, Nox nets opposing private flow, and only the unmatched aggregate touches Uniswap.

  • Private budget, direction, clip, limit and slippage.
  • Persistent encrypted state across epochs.
  • Official Uniswap V3 settlement remains unmodified.

Honest privacy boundary

NoxRoute does not claim invisibility. It protects the strategy.

ERC-20 deposits, wallet interactions and final aggregate settlements are still public. The value comes from protecting the strategy data observers usually exploit: direction, budget rules, per-user allocations, matched volume and remaining balance.

Why Nox belongs inside a swap flow.

Nox encrypts the swap intent before any strategy calldata is submitted: direction, budget, limit, slippage and per-epoch rules become private handles instead of plain public parameters.

That matters because Uniswap V3 and ERC-20 transfers are transparent by design. Nox lets users keep trading strategy private while still settling through the official public market: private matching and netting happen first, then only the aggregate settlement that must touch Uniswap is revealed on-chain.
